Legal & Contractual Status

Cuppan Insurance Consultants CC's representatives act on a full-time basis for the business. In terms of the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act, Cuppan Insurance accepts responsibility for its own actions and those of any representative mandated or contracted by it in rendering a financial service to a client.

Clients remain responsible for presenting full and correct information, and for making carefully considered decisions in the financial service process. Cuppan Insurance has no direct interest in any insurer or product, and receives less than 30% of its total income from any one insurer.

Compliance Arrangements

Cuppan Insurance Consultants CC uses an external compliance officer, Seshini Magan Compliance Services, Durban North.

Financial Services Offered

Cuppan Insurance and its representatives are authorised to market Short-Term Insurance — Personal Lines and Short-Term Insurance — Commercial Lines.

Professional Indemnity

Cuppan Insurance holds professional indemnity cover in place, underwritten through a licensed insurer. Cuppan Insurance does not collect cash premiums from clients; all contracted representatives and administrative personnel are covered under this policy.

Making a Claim

Should you need to claim, notify Cuppan Insurance as soon as possible, and within 30 days of the occurrence. For loss, theft, or an accident involving injury, the police should also be notified promptly. A claim form will be provided for completion and return, together with any supporting documents — our team will then assist you through the process.

Complaints

If you have a query about your policy, or are unhappy with the service you've received, you may contact our Compliance Officer (details available on request), your product supplier's compliance officer, the Ombudsman for Short-Term Insurance, or the FAIS Ombudsman, depending on the nature of the complaint.

Full Disclosure Document

The complete FAIS Disclosure Document — including key individual and representative details, compliance officer contact information, and full complaints procedures — is provided to every client individually as part of onboarding, in line with regulatory requirements. Please contact us if you'd like a copy.
