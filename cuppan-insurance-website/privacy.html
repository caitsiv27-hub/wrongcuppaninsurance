Privacy & POPIA

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

We understand that your personal information is important to you, and that you may be apprehensive about disclosing it. Your privacy is just as important to us, and we are committed to safeguarding and processing your information lawfully.

We also want to make sure you understand how, and for what purpose, we process your information. If you believe your information is not being processed correctly, or is being used for a purpose other than what it was originally intended for, you can contact our Information Officer, whose details appear below.

You can request access to the information we hold about you at any time. If you believe we hold outdated information, please ask us to update or correct it.

Why We Process Your Information

We collect, retain, use and disclose your personal information mainly to provide you with access to the services and products we offer. We only process your information for a purpose you would reasonably expect, including to:

  • Provide you with advice, products and services that suit your needs, as requested
  • Verify your identity
  • Issue, administer and manage your insurance policies
  • Process insurance claims and take recovery action where necessary
  • Notify you of new products or developments that may interest you
  • Confirm, verify and update your details
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

The information we may hold about you can include your name, contact details, physical and postal address, ID number, date of birth, occupation, qualifications, employment history, residency status, investments, assets, liabilities, insurance history, income, expenditure, and banking details where relevant to your policy.

Sharing Your Information

We may need to share your information to provide the advice, products or services you've requested. Where we share your information, we take precautions to ensure any third party treats it with the same level of protection we apply ourselves. Your information may be hosted on servers managed by a third-party service provider, which may be located outside South Africa.

Insurers also share information with each other regarding policies and claims, to help prevent fraudulent claims and assess risk fairly — this is done in the interest of all current and future policyholders, including via industry systems such as the Information Data Sharing System.

Formal Consent

When you request a quotation or take out a policy with us, you'll be asked to sign a formal POPI consent document authorising us to process and share your personal information for that purpose. Submitting a form on this website indicates your consent for us to contact you and begin that process — the full signed consent is completed as part of onboarding.
