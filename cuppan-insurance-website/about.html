Our Story

Since 1991, one family, one standard of service.

Cuppan Insurance Consultants CC has been serving South African clients since 1991, when our founder opened the practice in KwaZulu-Natal. The second generation joined the business in 2006, carrying that legacy forward.

In three decades, one thing hasn't changed: we believe insurance works best when it's built on a real relationship. Our clients stay with us for years because they get honest, expert advice from people who actually know them.

What Makes Us Different

Insurance matters most when the unexpected happens.

That's why our service goes beyond simply arranging cover. We're available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so when something goes wrong, you always have someone you know and trust to turn to. Whether you need guidance, assistance or simply someone to help you understand what happens next, we're there when you need us most.

It's personal service built around being there for our clients — not only when a policy is taken out, but throughout the journey.
